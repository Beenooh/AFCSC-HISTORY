Historical Overview

The Beginning

The Armed Forces Command and Staff College (AFCSC) was established to train officers in command and staff duties. The inaugural address by Gen. Olusegun Obasanjo marked the beginning of a new era in military professionalism.

"In The Beginning" - Inaugural address by Gen. Olusegun Obasanjo.

The Evolution

The evolution of the Nigerian Air Force has been defined by strategic leadership, technical proficiency, and operational readiness. From early training missions to modern drone maintenance and air power seminars.

Air Marshal S.K. Aneke, Chief of the Air Staff.
